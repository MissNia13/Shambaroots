# Season One — the locked shape

Decided Nov 3, 2026. Mark is CEO; this is his design, written down.

## Scope
One farm. The Kapsean centre. A tea-to-money loop. One first lead: Uncle Kamau.
Vehicles, dealership, open-world anything: out of Season One, not deferred, cut.

## Time
1 real second = 1 game minute. 06:00 start. Day/night drives what is open, not a timer.

## Money
One shared pot. KES for things with a price in the world; M-SHAMBA for game-side ledger.
No second wallet for convenience. If it costs, it comes out of the same place.

## Tea
Pick, carry, deliver. Leaf loses a quality band every day it is held.
Spoiled leaf is waste and waste cannot be sold. Leaving costs you: a missed delivery, a lost lead.

## The buyer
One buyer at the Kapsean centre sets the price by freshness band: 45 KES/kg fresh,
22 KES/kg stale, 0 for waste. No haggling in Season One. The clock is the only
negotiation: what you carry overnight is worth half at dawn, and what waits two
days is worth nothing.

## Training
Training is a real game-state lock. It costs the hours it costs, and the world moves while you sit in it.
Leaving mid-course loses what you paid and does not refund the time.

## First lead
Uncle Kamau is the way out of the greybox into the economy. His name and his clue text are Mark's to write.
Placeholder text is never allowed to reach canon.

## Camera
Fixed, low-poly, readable at a glance on a 4 GB laptop. No 3D open world.

## Balance rule
First run forgiving, second run should bite. Tune only after a stranger has played it.
